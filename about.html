About M.K ENTERPRISE

A Bhavnagar-based supplier of breathing air and fire-safety equipment, serving industry since 2020.

Our story

Breathing Air Equipment, Done Simply

M.K ENTERPRISE started in 2020 in Bhavnagar, Gujarat, supplying SCBA sets, breathing air cylinders and compressors. Over the years we have built strong experience in refurbishing and servicing breathing apparatus, and today we also supply brand new carbon fibre SCBA sets.

Our range covers Dräger, Scott, MSA and Sabre breathing apparatus, carbon and steel cylinders, and Bauer and Coltri compressors. We work directly with plants, contractors and fire-safety dealers across India — with honest advice, fair prices and quick replies straight from the owner.
