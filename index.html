Part selector

Know your set.

Cylinder

6.8 litre carbon fibre cylinder with valve, 300 bar working pressure and 450 bar test pressure. M18 × 1.5 thread, 15-year service life, and much lighter than a steel cylinder.

Mask
Full-face mask with a wide-view visor. The face seal and head harness hold it in place so the wearer breathes only the air from the set.
Demand Valve
Positive-pressure lung demand valve. It plugs into the mask and delivers air as the wearer breathes in.
Gauge
Pressure gauge on the shoulder strap shows the air left in the cylinder. The low-air warning whistle sounds when it is time to leave.
Harness
Backplate with padded shoulder straps and waist belt. A quick-release cylinder strap allows a fast cylinder change.

Product range

Breathing apparatus for plants, terminals and dealers.
