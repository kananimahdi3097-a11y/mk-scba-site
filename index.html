FAQ

Common Questions

What is an SCBA set?

A Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus gives the wearer clean breathing air from a cylinder carried on the back, so they can work safely in smoke, toxic gas or oxygen-deficient areas such as confined spaces.

How long does a 6.8 litre 300 bar cylinder last?

Roughly 45 minutes at a moderate work rate. Actual time depends on the user's breathing rate and the work being done.
